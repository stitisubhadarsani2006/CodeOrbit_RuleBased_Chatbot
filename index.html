The Rulebook

This bot has no AI model — it checks your message against these rules, top to bottom, and replies using the first one that matches.

  • Greetinghi, hello, hey
  • Farewellbye, goodbye, see you
  • Thanksthanks, appreciate it
  • Wellbeinghow are you
  • Identityyour name, who are you
  • Helphelp, what can you do
  • General questionany message ending in ?
  • Fallbacknone of the above
Rules are checked in the order shown above. The first match wins — later rules are never reached once one fires.
